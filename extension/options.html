Get one at typesafe.ai. Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and sent only to api.typesafe.ai. Only the sender, subject and a short preview of the emails on screen are sent to api.openai.com, once per inbox. Makes the Sort button instant. Costs about $0.015 in ranking calls each time the list of emails changes (at most once a minute). Only used so sentences like "what matters to me" have someone to mean. Leave blank and ranking goes by the sentence alone.
  • Emails are read from the Gmail page you have open: sender, subject, preview and (when available) the first part of the message.
  • Each email's text is sent to the ranking API to rank it. With an OpenAI key, senders, subjects and short previews are also sent to OpenAI to suggest sort ideas.
  • Sorting only rearranges what's on your screen. The only change to your account is "Move to Trash", which uses Gmail's own Delete button after you confirm, and Gmail's Undo reverses it.
